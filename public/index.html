Host

About the host.

Mehdi Nayebi
Host & Creator

Entrepreneur, operator, and host of Open Civilization.

Born in Tehran, raised in France, and shaped by building companies across Europe, the Middle East, and North America.

He co-founded Alopeyk, one of Iran's largest tech platforms, and ran it inside a sanctioned authoritarian system before leaving the country for his safety.

That's where this show comes from. He has seen what it looks like when a state works against the people trying to build.
